UEs

Each UE's keys are held by the UDM (write-only: they never leave it), its policy by the PCF (none of its own: the default). Bit rates in Mbit/s, uplink / downlink.

Add or replace a UE

A UE that exists is replaced whole: its keys, and its policy (none given: the default). Empty fields take the defaults: AMF field 8000, SQN 0, 5G AKA, the default policy's bit rates.

Advanced

Import UEs (JSON)

A list of UEs with the form's fields; each is added or replaced as above, missing fields taking the defaults; policy takes the PCF's fields (ue_ambr_mbps, sessions with their dnn, ambr_mbps, flows).
