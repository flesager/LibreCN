# 3GPP protocols

One crate per protocol or specification family, used by the NFs of `nf/`.
None of them knows the scheduler's EOs except `sbi`, whose messages are EO
message bodies.

| Crate | Specs | Content |
|---|---|---|
| [`ngap`](ngap/) | TS 38.413 | NGAP, ASN.1 APER, generated by `rasn-compiler`; see [`ngap/README.md`](ngap/README.md) |
| [`nas`](nas/) | TS 24.501, 24.007 | 5GMM messages of registration and NAS security, hand-written |
| [`security`](security/) | TS 35.206, 33.220, 33.501, 33.401 | Milenage, the 5G key derivations, 128-NIA2 and 128-NEA2 |
| [`sbi`](sbi/) | TS 29.509, 29.503 | the service-based interfaces as EO messages: 5G AKA between AMF, AUSF and UDM |

## NAS (`librecn-nas`)

- **No allocation**: decoding gives views into the received PDU
  (`mm::Uplink::decode`); encoding writes into a buffer the caller gives
  (`mm::Downlink::encode`). Overflow is reported once, at the end.
- **Only what the AMF needs**: the uplink messages of registration,
  authentication, security mode, identification and deregistration are
  decoded, their downlink answers encoded. Unknown optional IEs are skipped
  by their IEI format (TS 24.007 §11.2.4).
- **Security** (`security`): the protected-PDU header and the AMF side of a
  5G NAS security context: MAC check with NAS COUNT estimation, in-place
  deciphering, protection of downlink messages. NIA2 for integrity, NEA2 or
  NEA0 for ciphering; BEARER is 1 for 3GPP access, as UERANSIM checks it.
- **Robustness**: property tests feed random and half-valid PDUs to every
  decoder (`tests/robustness.rs`).

## Security (`librecn-security`)

RustCrypto (`aes`, `cmac`, `ctr`, `hmac`, `sha2`) under small functions on
fixed-size arrays. Test vectors: TS 35.208 test set 1 (Milenage), TS 33.401
128-EIA2 test set 1, and the 5G AKA key chain of UERANSIM's sample
subscriber computed by UERANSIM's own implementation (K_AUSF, RES*, K_SEAF,
K_AMF, NAS keys, NIA2, NEA2). NIA1/NEA1 (SNOW 3G) and NIA3/NEA3 (ZUC) are
not implemented: every UE supports NIA2 and NEA2.

## SBI (`librecn-sbi`)

Fixed-layout bodies (`zerocopy`) carrying what the 3GPP operation carries,
in its terms (SUPI or SUCI strings, serving network name, RAND, AUTN,
HXRES*...). Requests and responses are paired by `corr_id` and the
`REQUEST` / `RESPONSE` flags. An HTTP/2 binding can come later without
changing the NFs' logic.
